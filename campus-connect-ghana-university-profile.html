About University of Ghana

The University of Ghana, Legon is the oldest and largest of Ghana's public universities, offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across colleges including Humanities, Basic and Applied Sciences, Health Sciences, Education, and Business.

The main campus sits in Legon, Greater Accra, with satellite campuses supporting distance and evening programmes. Admission is competitive and based on WASSCE / SSSCE results and programme-specific cut-off points published each year.

Admission Requirements — Undergraduate

  • ✓WASSCE / SSSCE ResultsCredit passes (A1–C6) in six subjects including English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science, at one sitting.
  • ✓Programme-Specific Cut-OffsSome programmes (e.g. Medicine, Law) require higher aggregate scores — check the published cut-off list each admissions cycle.
  • ✓Valid Ghana Card / PassportRequired for identity verification during application.
  • ✓Application FeeGH₵200 non-refundable, paid online during submission.
  • ✓International ApplicantsEquivalent qualification assessment through the Admissions Office, plus an English proficiency test where applicable.
